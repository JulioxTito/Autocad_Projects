## Featured Projects

### 1. Commercial Facility Power Distribution & ATS Switchboard (SLD)

* **Directory:** [./01-commercial-power-distribution-sld](./01-commercial-power-distribution-sld)
* **Focus:** Low-voltage distribution, dual-source power switching, overcurrent protection, emergency loads.

#### System Architecture Overview
* **Dual Power Sources:** Primary utility entrance coupled with an emergency standby Diesel Generator (DG) supply via a 400A 4-pole bus coupler.
* **Redundancy & Transfer:** Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) units isolating and switching phase/neutral feeds to guarantee continuous power to critical infrastructure.
* **Overcurrent Protection:** Coordinated molded case circuit breakers (MCCB) and air circuit breakers (ACB) protecting individual downstream drops.
* **Downstream Feeders:**
  * Dedicated life-safety fire pump feeder (`F.PUMP PNL`).
  * Multi-zone facility lighting and tenant meter distribution panels.
  * Mechanical HVAC / chiller equipment feeds.
  * Designated spare feeder drops for future commercial expansion.
* **Cable Schedule:** Heavy industrial multi-core XLPE (Cross-linked Polyethylene) armored cable specifications (e.g., 3.5C XLPE aluminum conductors).
