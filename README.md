# AutoCAD Electrical & Infrastructure Projects

A collection of 2D computer-aided design (CAD) drawings and electrical system schematics developed in **AutoCAD**, highlighting facility infrastructure, low-voltage power distribution, Single-Line Diagrams (SLD), and life-safety systems.

---

## Featured Projects

* **Directory:** [./01-commercial-power-distribution-sld](./01-commercial-power-distribution-sld)

#### System Architecture Overview
* **Dual Power Sources:** Primary utility entrance coupled with an emergency standby Diesel Generator (DG) supply via a 400A 4-pole bus coupler.
* **Redundancy & Transfer:** Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS) units isolating and switching phase/neutral feeds to guarantee zero uninterrupted downtime for critical infrastructure.
* **Overcurrent Protection:** Coordinated molded case circuit breakers (MCCB) protecting individual downstream drops.
* **Downstream Feeders:**
  - Dedicated life-safety fire pump feeder (`F.PUMP PNL`).
  - Multi-zone facility lighting and general power distribution panels.
  - Mechanical HVAC / chiller equipment feeds.
  - Spare feeder drops for future commercial expansion.
* **Cable Schedule:** Heavy industrial multi-core XLPE (Cross-linked Polyethylene) armored cable specifications (e.g., 3.5C 300 mm²).
